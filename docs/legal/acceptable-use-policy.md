This policy describes the content and behaviour that are prohibited
when using BillLens. It applies to all users, including guest users.

## 1. Content you upload

You may upload receipt images, payment screenshots and written notes
for the purpose of organising shared expenses. You must not upload:

- content that is unlawful, fraudulent, or deceptive;
- content that infringes others' intellectual property or privacy;
- **another person's financial documents** without their permission
  (e.g. their card statements, their payment screenshots);
- malware or files disguised as images (BillLens treats all uploaded
  files as untrusted: MIME type and size are validated; executable
  content is rejected);
- sexual content, violent content, or content depicting minors;
- personal data of others beyond what shared expense bookkeeping
  requires.

## 2. Behaviour

You must not:

- create accounts or invitations for harassment, scams, or fraud;
- attempt to access groups, attachments, or data you are not invited
  to (technical probing is also prohibited);
- automate abuse of the Service (scraping, credential stuffing,
  invitation spam, payment-request spam);
- bypass rate limits, security controls, or audit mechanisms;
- misrepresent BillLens's output as proof of payment or proof of debt
  (BillLens records are bookkeeping, not legal proof);
- use the Service in violation of applicable financial, sanctions, or
  tax law (e.g. using settlement records to evade reporting duties).

## 3. Enforcement

Violations may result in content removal, feature restriction, account
suspension, or termination, and — where required or appropriate —
reporting to authorities. When feasible and lawful, we will notify the
affected user of enforcement action and the reason. You may appeal by
contacting support@billlens.app. [APPEAL PROCESS DETAIL — legal to
confirm.]

## 4. Reporting

Report abuse or security issues to:

- Abuse: abuse@billlens.app
- Security (do not publicly disclose before we respond):
  security@billlens.app — see `docs/legal/security-overview.md` for
  coordinated disclosure expectations.

## 1. What BillLens is

BillLens is an **expense organisation tool**. It helps you record,
split and explain shared expenses, and keep bookkeeping between people
who share costs.

## 2. What BillLens is NOT

- BillLens is **not a bank**, payment processor, money transmitter, or
  financial institution.
- BillLens **does not hold funds** at any time. Money you and your
  group members exchange moves entirely outside BillLens.
- BillLens **does not process payments**. Choosing the UPI option opens
  your device's UPI app with the amount pre-filled; the payment itself
  happens entirely within that external app, under that app's terms.
- **Opening an external payment app does not prove payment happened.**
  BillLens cannot see whether a transfer actually occurred, and it
  never marks a settlement as paid by itself.
- **You are responsible for verifying settlements** with the other
  person. The confirmation step in BillLens is a statement by the
  confirming person, not a verification by BillLens.

## 3. Receipt reading (OCR)

- BillLens reads receipt and screenshot images to propose draft fields
  (merchant, date, total, line items).
- **OCR may contain errors** — mis-reads, wrong totals, missed items,
  wrong currency or date interpretation.
- **You must review every extracted field before confirming.** Fields
  the app is unsure about are highlighted; the app requires your review
  before saving. Confirmed values become the record.

## 4. Balances and settlements

- Balances are derived from the expenses and settlements recorded in
  the app. If the recorded data is wrong (including OCR errors, missed
  expenses, or wrongly confirmed settlements), the balances will be
  wrong.
- Settlement confirmations and disputes are audit-logged so records can
  be reviewed and corrected.
- Debt simplification changes *who pays whom* — it never changes any
  member's net position.
